Private because it never leaves your device

Transcribed on your device

Speech recognition runs on your own processor. After a one-time download of the speech model (about 140 MB), it works with no internet at all.

No account, no cloud

There is nothing to sign up for and no server to send recordings to. The app has no analytics and no tracking.

Your files, in plain formats

Each meeting is a folder of audio and transcripts as text, Markdown and subtitles (SRT). On Windows they live in Documents\Superteem Voice.

Open source

Every line of the apps and the pin firmware is public and open source, so anyone can check what it does with your voice.

What it does

Voice notes

Start a note and talk. Your words are written into it as you go, ready to edit.

Meeting transcripts

Record from several microphones at once. You get a timecoded transcript with speakers recognised by voice, plus bookmarks and action items.

Voice commands

On Windows, say “Hey Superteem, start meeting”, “take a note” or “bookmark that” without touching the keyboard.

Voice Pin

Support for Seeed’s reSpeaker Clip, a wearable recorder that clips onto your clothes, is in development.

Transcription is in English for now.
