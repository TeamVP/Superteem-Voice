# Plutarch's Lives

By A. H. Clough.

Project Gutenberg EBook 674, Arthur Hugh Clough's 1859 revision of John Dryden's translation, prints no separate preface. After the title and the contents list, the text opens with the Life of Theseus.

The twenty-third traditional pair, Epaminondas and Scipio, is not in this ebook. Comparisons that do not survive are left out: Themistocles and Camillus, Pyrrhus and Caius Marius, Alexander and Caesar, and Phocion and Cato the Younger.
